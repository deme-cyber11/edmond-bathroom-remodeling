Bathroom Remodeling FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about our bathroom remodeling services in Edmond and the OKC metro.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Edmond, OK?

Bathroom remodels in Edmond typically range from $7,500 to $22,000. A basic refresh runs $7,500–$11,000, mid-range $11,000–$16,000, and full luxury remodel $16,000–$25,000+. We provide free in-home estimates.

How long does a bathroom remodel take in Edmond?

Most bathroom remodels take 2–4 weeks. Simple cosmetic updates finish in 1–2 weeks. Full gut-and-remodel projects with plumbing and tile take 3–5 weeks.

Do you handle permits with the City of Edmond?

Yes — we manage all City of Edmond building permits. Oklahoma requires permits for plumbing and electrical modifications in bathroom remodels.

Can you convert a tub to a walk-in shower?

Absolutely — tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most popular projects. We design custom tile showers, glass enclosures, and barrier-free options.

Do you serve Oklahoma City, Yukon, and Mustang?

Yes — we serve all of the OKC metro including Edmond, Oklahoma City, Yukon, Mustang, Piedmont, Norman, Moore, and surrounding communities.

More Questions About Your Remodel

Do I need to vacate my home during the remodel?

In most cases, no. Bathroom remodels are contained to a single room, so the rest of your home remains fully livable throughout the project. If you have two bathrooms, you can continue using the other while we work. For single-bathroom homes, we discuss the timeline upfront and work efficiently to minimize any inconvenience.

What tile and fixture brands do you work with?

We work with a wide range of quality brands including Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, Schluter, and Daltile. We can also source specialty tiles, custom vanities, and designer fixtures based on your preferences. Our team will help you find options that fit both your style and budget during the design consultation.

Can you work around an existing layout, or do you move plumbing?

Both are possible. Working within the existing layout keeps costs lower and reduces project time. However, if your goals require moving a toilet, adding a second sink, or relocating a shower, our licensed plumbers can handle those changes. We will clearly outline the cost and timeline difference so you can make the best decision for your home.

Is your work under any warranty?

Yes. We stand behind our craftsmanship with a one-year labor warranty on all completed work. Manufacturer warranties on fixtures and materials are passed through to you as well. If anything is not right after we finish, call us and we will make it right — no run-around, no excuses.
